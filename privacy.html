SwiftShop Privacy Policy

Updated: October 4, 2026

This policy describes personal information used by SwiftShop, including SwiftShop OW delivery and SwiftRide services. For privacy questions, contact dancu1213t@gmail.com.

Information used by the app

  • Customer and account information: your name, phone number, customer or ride-session identifiers, and information you enter in your profile or contact with support.
  • Orders and rides: requested items, quantities, prices, payment-method selection, delivery and pickup details, trip destinations, status history, notes, ratings, and applicable fees. Gift or group orders may include recipient or participant details that you provide.
  • Location: the delivery address or map pin and ride pickup and destination you select. With your device permission, the app accesses your location while you use location features to help select these points. The customer app does not request background location permission.
  • Notifications: if you enable phone notifications, a device push token and app-installation identifier are used to send delivery and ride updates to that device.
  • Device storage: the app stores settings, saved addresses, favourites, cart drafts, recent orders, notification history, and session information locally. Cart drafts expire after one day. Other local records can remain until you remove them, clear app storage, or uninstall.
  • Service records: our infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request timestamps, and error records when you connect to the service.

Why information is used

Information is used to operate customer accounts, arrange shopping and delivery, book and track rides, calculate and display fees, administer credit and rewards, deliver requested notifications, respond to support and deletion requests, and protect the service against misuse.

Who receives information

SwiftShop Dispatch, relevant stores, and the assigned runner or driver receive the details needed to fulfil your request. For example, an assigned driver needs ride pickup and destination details, and a runner needs delivery contact and location information. Information you add to a gift or group order should be shared with the permission of the people involved.

Supabase provides database, authentication, and server-function infrastructure. Google Maps provides maps, location search, and routing; Firebase Cloud Messaging provides Android push delivery. These providers process information needed to provide their services. When you choose an external link, WhatsApp, phone call, email, or device sharing option, information you choose to send is handled by that service under its own policy.

Permissions and your choices

You can refuse or revoke location and notification permissions in device settings. You can select a location manually instead of using GPS. Phone alerts can also be turned off from the app’s notification settings. Turning off notifications does not cancel an order or ride. You may contact support to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information.

Retention and deletion

Customer and service records are kept while needed to provide your account and requested services. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. After verifying account ownership, SwiftShop removes data that is no longer needed, including associated profile and notification registration data. Relevant transaction, payment, safety, dispute, or fraud-prevention records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve a specific issue. Support will explain any exceptions that apply to your request; retained records are limited to those purposes. Copies in provider backups may remain until the provider’s backup-retention cycle completes.

Removing the app or clearing device storage does not delete server records. See Delete your SwiftShop account and data for the request process, including a way to request deletion without installing the app.

Security

The app connects to its configured backend over HTTPS. Access controls and verification are used to restrict sensitive operations. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Do not include passwords, full payment-card details, or private keys in order notes or support emails.

Policy changes

Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Contact SwiftShop if you need clarification about this policy or how a specific service uses your information.

Promotions and announcements

Published offers appear in the app notification inbox and may appear as a small dismissible card. Promotional phone alerts are optional and require notification permission plus the promotions setting. We store your device push token and preference to deliver these alerts. Turning off promotional alerts does not turn off order or ride updates. Dismissed offers and notification read status are stored on your device.
