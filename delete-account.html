Delete your SwiftShop account and data

You can request deletion of your SwiftShop / SwiftShop OW account and associated personal data without installing the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Email dancu1213t@gmail.com with the subject “SwiftShop account and data deletion request”.
  2. Include the phone number associated with your account and state that you want the account and its associated personal data deleted. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.
  3. SwiftShop support will contact you to verify account ownership, confirm the scope of the request, and explain any records that must be retained.

Open deletion-request email draft

The link opens an email draft. Review it and send it from your email app. If no email app opens, copy the address above and email us directly.

What the request covers

Your customer profile and associated personal information, including relevant delivery and ride records and phone-notification registrations, are reviewed for deletion. Records needed for legal, financial, safety, dispute, or fraud-prevention reasons may be retained for those purposes. Support will explain any applicable exceptions. Copies in provider backups expire according to the provider’s retention cycle.

This request is not an immediate cancellation of an active order or ride. Contact support for help with an active service. Uninstalling SwiftShop does not delete your server account. Local device data can also be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling.

Inside the app, the same option is available from Profile → Support & privacy → Delete account & data.
